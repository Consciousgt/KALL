STEP 1: 2-D SPATIAL FOURIER DECOMPOSITION

For each discrete stepped frequency sample ω, the 2-D measured aperture wavefield s(x', y', ω) is decomposed into spatial plane-wave components via 2-D FFT:

S(kx, ky, ω) = FFT2D[ s(x', y', ω) ]
STEP 2: COHERENT PHASE BACK-PROPAGATION

The wavefield is back-propagated to the near-field reference plane Z1 via the exact round-trip dispersion relation:

kz = √( (2ω/c)² - kx² - ky² )
S_tilde = S(kx, ky, ω) · exp(+j · kz · Z1)
STEP 3: CUBIC SPLINE kz RESAMPLING (UPGRADE TO SHEEN ET AL. 2001)

This system implements Sheen et al.'s (2001) wideband reconstruction algorithm with a cubic-spline upgrade to their original linear interpolation step. Because kz is non-linear with frequency ω, data samples lie on nested spherical shells. Cubic spline mapping from the non-uniform kz grid to a uniform Cartesian grid reduces sidelobe phase errors across wide fractional bandwidths:

S_uniform(kx, ky, kz) = Interp1D[kz_nonuniform → kz_uniform, kind='cubic']
STEP 4: 3-D FAST INVERSE FOURIER TRANSFORM

Applying a 3-D IFFT over the uniform (kx, ky, kz) volume yields the fully focused 3-D complex reflectivity scene:

f(x, y, z) = IFFT3D[ S_uniform(kx, ky, kz) ]
